## Profile

I am a Mechanical Engineer with experience in product development, NPI, and manufacturing engineering, specializing in **complete mechanical design packages** — from concept through production-ready drawings. My work covers **sheet-metal fabrication, mechanical assemblies, and consumer product design**, with a focus on accuracy, manufacturability, and clear technical documentation.

Each project in this portfolio includes:

- 3D CAD models and assemblies (SolidWorks)
- Detailed fabrication and workshop drawings
- 2D flat patterns with bend allowances
- Bills of Materials (BOMs) and drawing revision control
- Manufacturing-ready documentation
